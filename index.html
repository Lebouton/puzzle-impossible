Frequently asked questions

Is the impossible puzzle really impossible?

Not technically: every piece has exactly one place. But the sky is almost uniform, so there are almost no visual clues. Only the shape of the pieces can help you.

How does the score work?

The timer starts when you touch your first piece. When you want to stop, click "Share your score" to get an image to send to your friends.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, no sign-up, directly in your browser, on computer or phone.

What is a monochrome puzzle?

A monochrome puzzle is a jigsaw whose image is a single colour, or nearly. With no pattern to recognise, you can only rely on the shape of the pieces. The Impossible Puzzle is an online version: a plain blue sky.

How do you solve a hard puzzle like this?

You have one clue: the shape of the pieces. Study the edges and the notches, join pieces in pairs, then bring the small groups together. There is no trick, which is what makes it so hard for adults and kids alike.
